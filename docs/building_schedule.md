# Building Operations Schedule & Calendar

**Facility**: Bangkok Commercial Tower  
**Time Zone**: Asia/Bangkok (UTC+7)  
**Document ID**: DOC-SCHED-2026-v1  

---

## 1. Standard Operational Hours

* **Main Building Operating Hours**: Monday through Sunday, **06:00 to 22:00 Bangkok Time**.
* **Tenant Office Working Hours**: **08:00 to 18:00 Bangkok Time**.
* **Public Atrium & Ground Lobby**: Open for access **06:00 to 22:00**. Access restricted after 22:00.

---

## 2. Permitted Off-Hours Equipment Operations (22:00 – 06:00)

Outside of standard operational hours (specifically between 22:00 and 06:00 Bangkok Time), strict energy conservation protocols apply:

### Authorized Overnight Machines (Permitted to run 24/7):
1. **`AC-S5` (Server Room & Data Center)**: 
   * Authorized for continuous **24/7 operation**. Must never be turned off.
2. **`FAN-01` (Basement Parking Ventilation)**:
   * Authorized for **continuous 24/7 ventilation** at baseline 40% speed for statutory air circulation and fire safety standards.
3. **`AC-L1` (Ground Lobby Unit)**:
   * Authorized between 22:00 and 00:00 in night setback mode (27°C setpoint), then shut down from 00:00 to 06:00 unless approved for overnight cleaning.

### Unauthorized Overnight Machines:
* **All Other Units (`AC-L2`, `AC-L3`, `AC-S1`, `AC-S2`, `AC-S3`, `AC-S4`, `FAN-02`, `FAN-03`, `FAN-04`)**:
  * **MUST BE OFF** between 22:00 and 06:00.
  * Any operation of these units at 03:00 AM constitutes an operational anomaly, unauthorized human manual override, or legacy schedule overrun from the manual operation period.

---

## 3. Floor-by-Floor Directory

* **Ground Floor / Zone A**: Main lobby, visitor reception, security desk, and building management office. Served by `AC-L1` and `FAN-02`.
* **Floors 1 to 3 / Zone B**: Open tenant corporate suites (`AC-S1`, `AC-S2`), common areas (`AC-L2`, `FAN-03`), and shared conference/meeting facilities (`AC-S3`).
* **Floors 4 to 6 / Zone C**: Executive offices (`AC-S4`), upper corporate tenancies (`AC-L3`, `FAN-04`), and secure data center (`AC-S5`).
